COO to price and card · **Card Todo:** to be opened · **Binding rules:**

## The defect

At M2-K23 the loader's document-led `Replace` step — and the
`jinn:profile-admin` `swap-plugin` that shares it —
(`crates/jinnd-loader/src/apply.rs`, `StepKind::Replace`) is
`dispose_step` then `spawn_step`. The old fiber rests `Disposed` under
`ExplicitDispose`: its world journal is withdrawn LIFO
(`LaneCore::withdraw_journal`), its listens are released outright (no
tombstone), and the successor is a NEW fiber on the new package's lane
whose activation is not staged. Two consequences the law reads the other
way (the M2-K4 ruling, `contracts/README.md` §Lifecycle classification,
R8, I4): the entry's fs writes are gone after a provider swap, and a
reply-expecting walk between the disposal and the successor's `listen`
selects nobody — the harness #47 shape, on the one swap every seam
proves. Pinned as a stated limit by
`crates/jinnd-daemon/tests/profile_admin.rs::a_swap_disposes_the_old_incarnation_a_stated_limit_until_m2_k27`
(the old incarnation's rest cause is `ExplicitDispose`, no
`FiberSuspended` row, a new fiber id); that test flips when this card
lands. The bundle README (`contracts/jinn-profile-admin/README.md`
§Limits) and constitution 04 §Reconcile semantics name the limit.

## Why M2-K23 could not close it (the STOP trigger, by the reading)

- `crates/jinnd-wasm/src/lane.rs`: a replacement activates STAGED only
  when `self.slot.ever_installed()` — the `SharedSlot` belongs to the
  `WasmBody`, i.e. to ONE fiber; a successor spawned by `spawn_step` is a
  new body with a fresh slot and never stages. The suspension that
  entombs listens and hands the journal back
  (`slot/teardown.rs::suspend`, `LaneCore::inherit`) runs only on the
  fiber engine's restart path of the SAME fiber, never on `dispose()`.
- `crates/jinnd-wasm/src/lane/commit.rs`: the staged commit replaces
  `core.topics.entombed(fiber)` — tombstones keyed by the fiber that
  entombed them; the card's entry-keyed tombstone set is `topics.rs`
  work, but the committing fiber is still a different fiber.
- The same-fiber alternative (swap the body's component under `restart`)
  changes the fiber's lane: `ReadinessProxy` is built at spawn from the
  OLD lane's `injects` and the fiber's readiness `signal` is fixed at
  `SpawnRequest` — re-pointing a live fiber's readiness gating is the
  engine's.

Either shape is the STOP rule's trigger 1: the fiber engine learning a
cross-fiber Reload (one fiber's suspension committed away by another
fiber's staged activation). The 150-minute round could not carry it
beyond the R1 and Law-2 fixes it was mandated; time is the second reason.

## Mechanism (recommended, to be priced)

1. **A replacement suspension the loader can ask for.** `EntryHandle`
   gains `replace(&self) -> KernelFuture<()>` (default-implemented as
   `dispose` for lanes without a suspend), backed by
   `Fiber::suspend` (exists: M2-K4) — the old fiber rests `Disposed`
   under `Suspend`, journal handed to the entry, listens entombed under
   `(entry, incarnation)`.
2. **Entry-keyed tombstones.** `LocalTopics::rebind` and `entombed` keyed
   by ENTRY (the K26 recommendation: "ONE `rebind` call replaces an
   entry's tombstones"); `LaneCore::end_tombstones` withdraws an entry's
   tombstones on the rest of the fiber that OWES them, never on the
   suspended predecessor's rest.
3. **A staged successor on a fresh slot.** `WasmBody::activate` stages
   when the ENTRY has tombstones or a retained journal, not when its own
   slot was ever installed; `commit.rs` commits by entry.
4. **The `Replace` step** = `replace` (suspend, awaited by the
   document-led path; scheduled by the admin chain as M2-K23 has it) then
   `spawn_step`; the K9 oracle answers `restarting` for the entry from
   `entombed_incarnation` across the gap (exists: M2-K26 (c)).
5. **Loom model, priced:** one model in `jinnd-wasm` (`--features loom`)
   over three actors — the predecessor's suspend replay, the successor's
   staged commit, a reply-expecting walk — asserting the walk sees
   tombstones (refused) before the commit and live listeners after, never
   neither, and that the entry's journal is withdrawn exactly once by
   whichever fiber rests last. ≈ 150 lines, loom-only (outside R10).
