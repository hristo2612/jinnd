## The defect

**The mechanism, by code at pin `a53a352`.** A `ConfigChanged` restart
runs through the fiber engine, and every unload that is not a disposal
SUSPENDS the live activation (`crates/jinnd-fiber/src/supervisor.rs`,
`unload`; M2-K4). The lane's suspend path
(`crates/jinnd-wasm/src/slot/teardown.rs`, `SeatState::suspend`)
UNLISTENS every `Listen` registration in the seat's journal, LIFO, then
disposes the instance — before any replacement exists. The replacement's
`WasmBody::activate` (`crates/jinnd-wasm/src/lane.rs`) instantiates a
LIVE seat (`staging: false`), so its `listen` lands in the topic table
the moment the guest reaches that call, and not before. Between the two,
`LocalTopics::emit` (`crates/jinnd-wasm/src/topics.rs`) selects from a
table with NO row for the topic; M2-K9's `doomed()` is asked about
selected listeners only, so it is asked about nobody; a waterfall
answers its own payload and traces `listeners: 0`. M2-K9's refusal is
unreachable on this path by construction — it is keyed on a SELECTED
listener owing a transition, and a withdrawn registration is never
selected.

Harness packet UI-2 (PLA-353), proof 5 of
`tests/composition/tests/moments.rs`: the operator edits the extension's
`source` through the document lane; the new source's activation is slow
by construction (~1.5 s); the client posts a moment every ~5 ms across

## North-star fit

R9: no silent replacement — a restart that silently un-subscribes its
listener replaces "validated" with "not validated" and says nothing. Law
2: the only trace is `listeners: 0`, and it is the same row an honest
empty topic writes. I4: between two rests the composition CLAIMS the
extension is subscribed (the profile says so, the entry is not disposed)
and it is not; the quiescent state after the edit is right, the path to
it is a history the record cannot tell from "no extension". R8: Mode 0
must be as honest as Mode 1's commit. M2-K9's own contract text
(`plugin.wit`, `events.emit`: "if any selected listener sits in an
incarnation that already owes a transition, the whole walk is refused")
promises a refusal the kernel cannot deliver on this path — the kernel
breaks its own WIT. Law 1, for #49: "capability authority arrives only
as grants checked at the broker … every call through one is refused
without its grant" (`plugin.wit`, world `plugin`); an ungated `emit` is
a side door the world's own preamble forbids.

## The contract delta

**(a) A listen registration outlives its instance's suspension as a
TOMBSTONE until the restart commits.** On a suspend that is a REPLACEMENT
— the fiber owes `Reload`: a `ConfigChanged`, `DependencyChanged` or
`ExplicitRestart` unload — the seat's `Listen` registrations are NOT
unlistened. Each becomes a tombstone: the same row (topic, context,
fiber) with no delivery target. A tombstone is SELECTED exactly as the
registration was (the same selector interrogation of the same context),
and a reply-expecting walk that selects one is refused whole by the
M2-K9 rule already in `emit`, naming the entry, the incarnation and what
it owes — `restarting` for the whole window, `Pending` and `Loading`
alike (d). Never delivered, never skipped, never queued (the M2-K9 law:
buffering hides a transition). A daemon-shutdown suspend (`Suspend`
cause) entombs too and refuses `suspended` — harmless and honest. A
DISPOSE (`retire`) withdraws outright as today: a disposed entry listens
to nothing (I1). A fiber M2-K25 fails for a dead instance retires, not
suspends, so a dead listener leaves no tombstone: its silence is the
truth, on the record.

**(b) The replacement commits ATOMICALLY, in the shape R8 already has.**
The replacement incarnation's activation runs as a STAGING seat — its
`listen`s and `provide`s recorded, not routed, exactly as `lane_swap`
stages — and its commit is `commit_staged`'s: under ONE topic-table lock
the entry's tombstones are removed and the staged listens registered
(`rebind`), the slot installed, provisions provided, alarms armed. Mode
0 gets Mode 1's commit. No walk ever sees "neither": before the lock it
selects tombstones (refused); after, live listeners. The commit lands
inside `activate`, before the engine commits `Active`; a walk between the
two is refused `restarting` by the existing M2-K9 rule (the new
incarnation owes its `Active`), which is honest and lasts microseconds.
The FIRST activation of an entry is unchanged: nothing is being
replaced, no tombstone exists, and the M2-K9 "arriving, not leaving"
rule holds.

**(c) A tombstone lives EXACTLY as long as the fiber owes a transition
(I4).** When the replacement FAILS (`Failed` committed) or the entry is
disposed, the tombstones are withdrawn — by the lane on the fiber's
committed rest, through the per-fiber state forwarder it already runs
(`crates/jinnd-wasm/src/lane/state.rs`, `track_states`) — each
withdrawal an `EffectWithdrawn { label: "listen <topic>" }` row, so the
record shows when the subscription actually ended and not when its
instance died. A walk that selects a tombstone the oracle no longer
explains (the instant between the rest commit and the withdrawal) is
refused `stalled`: the truth for a `Failed` fiber (R9 — nothing is
coming until the environment moves), never a delivery to nobody. The
quiescent state after a failed replacement is an entry `failed` with no
listener, which is what a fresh boot of the same profile produces (I4).
Whether a transport should refuse sends while its validator is `failed`
is the TRANSPORT's policy, readable off `jinn:introspect` — the UI-2
card's question, not the kernel's.

**(d) The oracle's in-flight arm.** `owed.rs`'s allowlist gains one arm:
a committed `Loading` under a non-terminal cause answers `Reload` — a
load in flight IS a scheduled replacement; its clean landing is `Active`
and its unclean landing is `Failed`, committed before its cleanup (the
M2-K9 round-4 ordering law), so the promise is never stale. Introspect
and the walk keep reading one source. The crate's property tests pin
both directions as they do today: never `Reload` where no load is
reachable or in flight; never `Stalled` mid-load. This is a derivation
change with no new interleaving; the supervisor and steering are
untouched.

**(e) #49 rides: `emit` is covered by the topic's grant exactly as
`listen` is.** `check_grant(grant_for(topic))` after the reservation
refusal and before selection; the refusal is the broker's own
`GrantRefused` row (`crates/jinnd-wasm/src/broker/authority.rs`) and the
guest's `grant-refused` — existing vocabulary, no new kind. Kernel
publishes (`crates/jinnd-wasm/src/topics/publish.rs`) do not pass
through `emit` and are unaffected. **Why it rides, with reasons.** (1)
Same seam, same two files (`surfaces.rs`, `topics.rs`), same verifier
fixture — two guests and a ledger read — so a second card would build
the fixture twice. (2) Zero design freedom: constitution 01 v0.1 and
`plugin.wit` already state the rule; `emit` merely lacks the check, and
the ONE decision of this card stays (a)–(c). (3) It cannot hide: its own
red-first case (case 7), its own commit, its own line on the meter
(§Size). (4) The pin-bump that adopts M2-K26 must audit every emitter in
the distribution once; two cards would make that two audits. **Why not,
answered.** It is an authority TIGHTENING: a guest that emits ungranted
today is refused tomorrow. R12 says never breaking within a major — the
world is 0.x, and the rule was law in prose before this card (the
M2-K13 precedent added "no guest may ever emit on a reserved topic" as a
rule at 0.8.0 with no signature change). Every first-party emitter in
the harness already carries its grants: the UI-2 card granted the
transport its three topics "so the profile already reads as the kernel
will one day enforce it". The wasm and daemon crates' own test guests
that emit ungranted are implementer-owned fixtures and gain grants; a
`tests/invariants/` case that emits ungranted is the verifier's — named
in round 1, never edited by the implementer (two-key).

**The ONE design decision — what a registration is in the window.**
Four shapes. (i) Nothing, as today: #47. (ii) Queue walks across the
restart: rejected by M2-K9 already — an unbounded queue hides a real
transition, and a queued waterfall answers a payload the sender has
moved past. (iii) Keep the OLD instance live and routed until the
replacement is healthy, Mode 1 for config restarts: rejected — it
doubles the cost of every config edit, delivers to an instance whose
CONFIG is stale (exactly what M2-K9 refuses), and changes restart
semantics for every lane. (iv) Tombstone plus atomic commit: chosen —
the refusal is M2-K9's existing vocabulary, the commit is R8's existing
shape, nothing is queued and nothing is stale. Recommended shape: the
tombstone is a `Listener` row whose target is absent, keyed so ONE
`rebind` call replaces an entry's tombstones with its staged listens;
the lane records which registrations it entombed at suspend beside the
world effects it already retains per entry. Rejected: a second table of
"fibers in replacement" consulted per topic (two tables for one fact,
R10).

**`Emit`-mode walks skip tombstones.** The fire-and-forget mode is not
decided by a pending transition (M2-K9's scope; `plugin.wit`: "`emit` …
is unaffected by THAT refusal"), so a notification emitted inside the
window is lost as it is today. NAMED LIMIT, not closed here: counting
that loss on the trace is a `DispatchTrace` field, a facade delta this
card does not authorize (§Out).

instance's suspension as a refusing registration until the successor

`docs/constitution/04-profiles.md` §Reconcile semantics gains one
sentence: a restart's window is closed to reply-expecting walks — a
listener's subscription is replaced, never absent. Profile schema: NONE.

1. `a_reply_expecting_walk_inside_a_config_restart_is_refused_restarting_never_answered_unmodified`
   — the #47 shape: a source edit with a slow replacement; every walk in

   { label: "listen …" }` AFTER it; walks after that select nobody (the

4. `a_disposed_entry_leaves_no_tombstone` — I1/I4: dispose during and

5. `an_in_flight_load_answers_restarting_on_introspect_and_on_the_walk`

6. `an_emit_mode_walk_in_the_window_is_lost_and_traced_as_today` — the

7. `an_emit_without_the_topics_grant_is_refused_on_the_record` — (e):
   `GrantRefused { contract: "<topic>" }`, `grant-refused` to the guest,

8. `the_mode_1_swap_is_unchanged` — the ratchet: the swap path's

9. `a_first_activation_is_never_refused` — the ratchet on M2-K9's

## Invariants lane — the two-key rule, stated

Verifier-owned cases land on **`invariants/m2-k26`**, a verifier-lane
branch, as a separate PR AFTER the implementation lands — never on the
packet branch. The repo enforces it: `.github/scripts/check-push-two-key.sh`
refuses a push touching both `tests/invariants/` and implementation
crates without an independent verifier author, and every implementer
session here commits under one identity. The verify node runs the cases
from `invariants/m2-k26` inside its own checkout on the packet head; after
the implementation merges, the verifier fast-forwards the lane onto
`main` and pushes it as its own invariants-only push (label
`verifier-approved`). A `tests/invariants/` case that (e) newly refuses
is the verifier's to amend on that lane; the implementer names it in
round 1 and touches nothing. A merge-conflict resolution touching
`tests/invariants/` takes the verifier's side byte-for-byte or STOPs
(AGENTS.md). The CI check is never weakened.

## Kernel findings this packet is likely to file

- `rebind` is keyed by listener ids; an entry's tombstones must be
  addressable as a set at commit — the record the lane keeps at suspend
  is the shape to get right (I1: exactly those, no other entry's).
- The staged restart activation and `commit_late`: a wake or call
  landing between the staged `activate` returning and the commit must
  not register into a seat that is not yet installed (`SharedSlot::extend`
  hands it back today; the ordering must stay exact).

  restart: the refused incarnation must be the one the caller must wait

- The daemon's shutdown suspend: tombstones must not delay quiescence or
  the ledger flush; a refused walk during shutdown is `suspended`.
