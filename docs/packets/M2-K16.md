## What to build — the design is given, do not re-derive it

kernel-dev's own proposal, produced while fixing M2-K14, verbatim:

> A contract assertion should not be free-text at all: give the test a helper
> that takes `(source, item-kind, name)` and locates the declaration
> **structurally** — anchored at line start for a top-level item, scoped to
> the enclosing block for a field — so `contains` over contract text becomes
> *unavailable* rather than merely discouraged.

Plus the requirement instance five added: **a contract file is asserted by
PARSING it wherever a format exists** — WIT through `wit-parser`, TOML
through `toml`, the Rust facade through `syn`; all already in the lockfile,
zero new packages. **Where no format exists — version references and row
shapes written inside comments and READMEs are OUR convention, not a
format — the lens is the ONE reader of that convention: its grammar is
stated in its doc, it is SCOPED (a prose assertion binds to a named item's
doc block, never to "any comment in the file"), and it FAILS CLOSED — a
candidate it cannot read is reported as a disagreement, never skipped,
proven red-first.** (COO ruling 2026-09-02 after round 1; the original
sentence read "never by matching text" and was over-broad — comments have
no parser, so a single fail-closed reader is the derived-copy design, and a
silently-skipping one is the vacuity in a new place.)

Three gates, one mechanism:

1. **Version references** in comments inside `wit/` and
   `contracts/*/contract.wit` agree with that file's own `package`
   declaration — asserted as a parsed package version, not a string.
   Red-first against instance 1 (restore the defect in a fixture, watch it
   fire).
2. **Row shapes** declared in `contracts/*/metadata.toml` agree with the
   facade type they describe — a parsed field list compared against the
   facade's own definition, never a hand-copied sentence.
3. **Unanchored substring assertions over contract text are unexpressible**:
   the helper is the only sanctioned way to assert contract content in tests,
   and a gate (clippy lint config, a scan, or the type system — the
   implementer chooses and justifies) fails the build on a bare `contains`
   over a contract constant. The M2-K14 sweep bounds the backlog: 123 asserted
   literals, 16 declaration-shaped, 2 unfirable — migrate them all.

**Sweep before claiming green.** Run each gate across every contract bundle
and report the count of disagreements found, including zero WITH the sweep's
output shown. A gate whose first run finds nothing on a codebase that just
produced six instances deserves suspicion, not confidence.

## Evidence standard — inherited, not restated

Checkable red-first per gate, against the real historical defect where one
exists. Mutation-verified: **for each mutation, the file:line changed, the
exact command, and the verbatim failing output, posted as a Todo comment.**
`pass` = 0 Blockers; a behaviour-free Major is named in the verdict and fixed
before land. The repo is PUBLIC: zero personal data or machine paths in any
push. Two independent final-head workspace runs, separation named.

THREAT MODEL: honest drift between two hand-maintained copies under ordinary
editing, and an assertion author who believes a substring match is a proof.
Not an adversary editing a contract to misrepresent it.

STOP RULE: 2 rounds. If the structural-helper mechanism is what blows the
budget, say so in the round-1 report and the COO splits the packet rather
than letting a round overrun quietly. A third round is not available.

## COO rulings — 2026-09-02, after round 1 (binding; round 2 is the LAST round)

Round 1 built the lens, three gates red-first on their shipped instances,
swept every bundle (30 refs / 27 rows / 396 sources, 0 disagreements after 8
contract fixes), found **instance 7** (`contracts/jinn-introspect/contract.wit`
had never parsed: `from` is a WIT keyword, `record readiness` collided with
`readiness: func`), migrated all 34 substring sites, posted ten mutations.
Four Blockers came back; each is ruled here.

1. **"Still asserts with `contains` and hand-written readers" — PARTLY
   upheld, and the card was over-broad.** Formats are parsed (WIT, TOML,
   syn) — that part is done. Comment-borne version references and row
   shapes have no parser; the lens is the single reader of our own
   convention, which is the derived-copy design this packet exists for.
   **Round 2 must:** (a) make `refs.rs` and `rows.rs` FAIL CLOSED — a
   comment token that looks like a reference/row but does not read cleanly
   is a reported disagreement, not a skip — with a red-first test for each;
   (b) SCOPE `prose().states(..)` to a named item's doc block
   (`docs_of(item).states(phrase)` or equivalent) so a phrase can never be
   satisfied by an unrelated comment elsewhere in the file, and name it for
   what it proves (prose presence in that block, nothing more); (c) state
   each reader's grammar in its doc. No rewrite.
2. **R12 on `readiness` → `readiness-report` — upheld; the fix is a
   version, not a pretence.** The rename is forced (one namespace, and the
   function IS the wire operation), but a rename at an unchanged version is
   not additive whatever the file's history. **Bump `jinn:introspect` 0.4.0
   → 0.5.0**, with a dated line in the contract header: 0.4.0 never parsed
   (instance 7), 0.5.0 is the first parseable edition, the wire operation
   `readiness` and its answer shape are unchanged. Sweep every
   `jinn:introspect@` reference (gate 1 will) and the bare "0.4.0" mentions
   in `wit/plugin.wit` and the daemon doc by hand — gate 1's known limit is
   bare version numbers; say so in the report. `%from` stands.
3. **Lens budget 1332 vs 1200 — ACCEPTED, re-priced to ≤ 1400** on the
   declared meter (see the header). Round 2's additions must fit; if they do
   not, that is the split signal and you say so rather than trim.
4. **Mutation evidence for M2b, M3, M8, M9 — upheld.** Post the verbatim
   failing output for each, re-run at the round-2 head; the form is the
   evidence. Correct the Minor (396, not 395) in the same comment.

**If round 2 fails on item 1 alone,** the packet does not get a third
round: the lens lands with items 2–4 closed and the reader semantics split
