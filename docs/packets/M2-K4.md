## The adjudicated defect (decision-log entry 2026-08-28)

At 41cb2f4 a clean SIGINT withdraws every fs mutation the process's fibers
made (state, history appends, reports revert to activation-time content),
while a SIGKILL preserves them — crash keeps state, graceful shutdown
erases it. Root cause is a conflation: **effect withdrawal is bound to the
fiber INCARNATION, but a contribution belongs to the profile ENTRY.** The
LAW's LIFO teardown (§3) governs *removal from the composition*; it says
nothing about a process exiting or an incarnation being replaced while the
entry persists in the profile.

## Ruling (the card implements exactly this)

1. **Dispose = removal.** When an entry is removed from the composition
   (profile removal, explicit dispose), the full LIFO withdrawal of that
   entry's contribution runs — unchanged, M1-P9b law intact.
2. **Shutdown = suspend.** Daemon shutdown stops fibers cleanly
   (quiescence, alarm cancellation of kernel-held resources, ledger flush)
   but withdraws NO durable effects — the composition persists in the
   profile, so its contributions persist on disk. The ledger records a
   typed suspension event per fiber (Law 2: what happened is "suspended",
   not "withdrawn"). Crash and clean shutdown now agree on disk outcome;
   clean shutdown additionally reaches quiescence and flushes.
3. **Incarnation replacement = continuity.** A reconcile/config-edit
   restart replaces the fiber but the entry persists: the successor
   incarnation inherits the entry's live effect journal (the durable
   retention store already survives the process — this is its purpose).
   Undo/revert of an inherited effect still works by key; the LIFO trail
   spans incarnations within the entry's lifetime. Hot-swap's seat-outcome
   continuity is the precedent; reconcile joins it.
4. **Kernel-held resources are NOT durable effects.** Alarms, listeners,
   provided-service registrations die with the process/incarnation by
   nature and are re-established on activate (the jinn:clock contract
   already documents this honestly). The distinction is: withdrawal undoes
   *world* mutations; suspension releases *kernel* registrations. The
   effect machinery must classify accordingly — no undo of a world
   mutation on suspend, no orphaned kernel registration either.
5. **FINDINGS #15 (journal-seal escape):** first reproduce (dispose during
   a guest's `handle-event` mid-tick), then close the tear: after a
   fiber's journal seals for withdrawal, new effect registrations REFUSE
   with a ledgered per-entry error (fail-closed, same doctrine as grant
   admission) — a dispose trail is exactly the fiber's contribution (I1),
   never a prefix of it.
