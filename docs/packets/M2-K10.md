## Why now

K9 closed the *addressable-while-restarting* window. The harness then hit
the next one, and this time with a transcript: the settings owner
re-declares on every alarm wake, which is a call **into** the provider that
is at that moment dispatching a `changed` notice **into** the owner. Each
parks on the other and both die on the guest deadline. `PATCH
/v1/settings/cron` now answers (K9's work), but the deeper cycle remains and
the harness carries a named `#[ignore]` because of it.

Two facts the harness established that this card must respect:
- **`Emit` is not an escape.** The kernel awaits every listener delivery
  end-to-end in every mode; fire-and-forget discards the *answer*, never the
  *wait*. Any fix that assumes an emit returns early is wrong.
- **Whether the loser recovers is incidental.** One transcript showed the
  deadlocked fiber never recovering; a second showed it recovering, but only
  because that patch happened to target the fiber's own entry so the loader
  already owed it a restart. A fix must not rely on the loader happening to
  owe a restart.

## Scope

- **Detect the cycle, refuse the edge.** A reply-expecting dispatch that
  would close a cycle — the target is, transitively, already awaiting the
  caller — REFUSES immediately with a typed reason naming both ends, rather
  than parking until the guest deadline. Refusal is ledgered like any other.
- **The refusal carries the caller's next move**, per the K9 vocabulary: a
  cycle is not a restart and not a scope error; it is its own reason and the
  caller must not blindly retry.
- **Observable, not inferred:** `jinn:introspect` exposes enough of the
  wait graph that an operator can see why a call refused. Additive per R12.
- Red-first: reproduce the harness's exact shape — owner re-declares into a
  provider that is mid-notify into the owner — and assert a typed refusal
  inside the deadline, on both orderings.

## Round protocol
Standard kernel-packet rounds; verifier owns invariant additions (candidate:
`a-dispatch-that-would-close-a-cycle-refuses`). Hostile probes: three-fiber
cycles; a cycle that forms concurrently with a restart; a self-dispatch;
refusal storms; the ordering where the second edge arrives first.

## Acceptance
- Fixture through the real daemon reproducing the harness two-way shape:
  typed cycle refusal within the guest deadline, ledgered and distinguishable
  from `Restarting` and from a scope refusal, on both orderings; introspect
  shows the waiting edge.
- The harness's `#[ignore]`d settings recovery test passes at the bumped pin
  — verified by actually running it, not by assuming.
- Ratchet: no regressions; gates + loom + miri (non-FFI) + `--locked`;
  two-key clean; ceilings per this card; 300-line cap (hard for src, soft for
  coherent test suites per R10).
- Contract deltas additive and versioned (R12).

## Out of scope
